# Forest Stock Mapping Carbon Monitoring Pipeline

## Executive Summary
A scalable, reproducible, and open-source Remote Sensing & GIS pipeline for forest inventory and carbon accounting. Six phases take a Sentinel-2-style scene from raw bands to an IPCC-converted CO2e stock map with plot-level ground truth, and every stage is reproducible from embedded seeds and hashes.
